# Leafocus Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026
**Last updated:** 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Leafocus mobile app ("App") processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can use your rights. Users in Türkiye should also read the [KVKK Information Notice](kvkk-aydinlatma-metni.md) (in Turkish). Section 10 covers the GDPR for users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom.

## 2. Summary
- If you use the App as a **guest**, your data stays on your phone. No personal data reaches us.
- If you **create an account**, your e-mail address and the content you create are stored on our server to back them up and sync them between your devices.
- No ads. No analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
- We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
- You can delete your account and all your data from inside the App or on the web.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Guest use
Everything you create as a guest (spaces, folders, plans, to-dos, focus sessions, notes, settings) is stored only in the local database on your device and is not sent to our servers.

When guest mode is chosen for the first time, an **anonymous counter** on our server is increased by one. The counter holds nothing that can identify you (no ID, device number or e-mail). It only tells us how many people use guest mode. As with any internet request, our hosting provider may keep your IP address in short-lived technical logs (see 3.3).

### 3.2 When you create an account
| Data | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and contact | E-mail address, display name (if given, otherwise the part of the e-mail before @) | Account creation, sign-in, verification and password reset codes |
| Account security | Password (stored only as a one-way hash; we never see it in plain text), session tokens | Secure sign-in |
| App content | Space and folder names, colour/icon choices, plans, to-dos, notes, end-of-session notes | Backing up your data and syncing it between your devices |
| Usage records | Start/end time and length of focus and break sessions, and the folder they belong to | Statistics, streak and history screens; sync |
| Plan status | Whether you are Pro | Unlocking Pro features |

**About note content:** You decide what you write in your notes. Please do not write special-category data (health, religion, political opinions, etc.) or other people's personal data in notes. Such content is not processed in any special way. It is stored only as the text you wrote.

### 3.3 Technical data
For each request the App makes to the server, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs such as IP address, request time and client information, for security and troubleshooting. We do not use these logs for advertising or profiling.

### 3.4 Data that stays on the device
- **Notifications:** Timer and session-end notifications are created locally on your device, not sent from a server.
- **Home screen widget:** Information shown in the widget is kept on your device.
- **Device backup:** If Android's own backup is on, the operating system may back up the App's local data to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

### 3.5 What we do not collect
Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, advertising ID, device ID, payment card details. (If in-app purchases are added later, Google Play / the App Store process the payment and your card details never reach us. This policy will be updated then.)

## 5. Who we share data with
We do not sell your data. It is shared only with these service providers, who work on our behalf and are needed to run the service:

| Provider | Purpose | Data location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase Inc. (infrastructure on Amazon Web Services) | Database, authentication, server | EU, Ireland (eu-west-1) |
| Google LLC (Gmail) | Sending verification and password reset e-mails | Google servers (including the USA) |
| GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft) | Storing an encrypted backup of the database (unreadable to GitHub, deleted after 30 days) | USA |
| Google Play / Apple App Store | Distributing the App (the store also processes data under its own policy) | The respective company's servers |

Data may also be disclosed to competent public authorities where the law requires it.

## 6. International transfers
Our server is in Ireland (EU). Because Google is used to send e-mails, your e-mail address and the content of the e-mail sent to you may be processed on Google servers, including in the USA. Transfers from the EEA rely on safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR (for example the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). For users in Türkiye, the transfer relies on the explicit consent you give when you create an account (KVKK Article 9); if you do not consent, you can use the App as a guest. See the [KVKK Information Notice](kvkk-aydinlatma-metni.md) for details.

## 7. Retention
| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account and content data | While your account is open |
| Individual items you delete | Kept with a "deleted" mark so the deletion reaches your other devices; removed entirely when you delete your account |
| When you delete your account | Deleted from the live database immediately. Any copies in the provider's technical backups are deleted automatically within 30 days at the latest |
| Technical logs | A short period set by the provider, at most 30 days |
| Verification e-mails sent | At most 30 days in the sending mailbox |
| Guest data | Only on your device. Removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data |

If there is a legal dispute or an official request, the relevant data may be kept until it is resolved.

## 8. Security
- All traffic between the App and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
- Your password is never stored in plain text.
- The database uses row-level security (RLS): each user can access only their own data. Critical fields such as Pro status cannot be changed from the App.
- No system is 100% secure. If a data breach occurs, we will notify the competent authorities and, where required, you, within the time limits set by the GDPR and the KVKK.

## 9. Your rights
You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to receive a copy of your data (portability), and to claim compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully.

- **Deletion:** In the App, *Settings → Permanently delete my account and data*. If you cannot access the App, see the [Account Deletion page](hesap-silme.md).
- **Correction:** You can edit your content inside the App.
- **Other requests:** E-mail support.leafocus@gmail.com from the address registered to your account. We answer free of charge within **30 days** at the latest.

You may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or, in the EEA/UK, with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

## 10. EEA and UK users (GDPR)
- The controller is the person named in section 1. We have no representative in the EU; if one becomes required under Article 27 of the GDPR, this policy will be updated.
- Legal bases are in section 4, recipients in section 5, transfers in section 6, retention in section 7 and your rights in section 9.
- We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.
- Providing data is not mandatory. An e-mail address and password are needed to create an account. Without them you can still use the App as a guest.

## 10A. Users in the USA and other countries
- **We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** There is no advertising and no tracking in the App.
- If you live in a US state with a privacy law (for example California, Virginia, Colorado), you have the right to know what we hold about you, to access, correct and delete it, to receive a copy, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Use the contact in section 9. We verify requests through your registered e-mail address and answer within 30 days. You may also appeal a refusal by writing to the same address.
- If your country's law (for example Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Switzerland's FADP) gives you more rights than this policy, we also respect those rights.
- Because the controller is located in Türkiye, data stored on our server may be accessed from Türkiye.
- The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data (see section 11).

## 11. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If your country sets a higher age for digital services (for example up to 16 in EU countries) and you are below it, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.
